CAT CAM - be a cat in your video calls
=======================================

INSTALL (once)

1. Drag "Cat Cam" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Install OBS Studio from https://obsproject.com (it provides the virtual
   camera Cat Cam sends your cat to). Then, once:
   - Open OBS and click "Start Virtual Camera".
   - If macOS asks, allow the system extension (System Settings >
     General > Login Items & Extensions).
   - Click "Stop Virtual Camera" and quit OBS.
   Keep OBS closed while you use Cat Cam.

3. Open Cat Cam from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't
   check the app (it isn't from the App Store). Click "Done", then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
   next to Cat Cam, and confirm. You only do this once.

4. Allow camera access when macOS asks, and microphone access too.

5. A few keyboard shortcuts also work while Zoom is in front. For those,
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring and switch
   on Cat Cam (if it isn't listed, click + and choose Cat Cam from
   Applications). Then quit Cat Cam and open it again.

6. Optional, for barks and meows instead of your voice in the call:
   the free BlackHole 2ch virtual microphone, made by Existential Audio.
   No Terminal needed:
   - Open Cat Cam and click "Get the voice" (bottom right corner of its
     window). Cat Cam downloads BlackHole from its makers' website, checks
     it, and opens its installer.
   - Click through the installer. It asks for your Mac's password (it
     installs an audio driver).
   - Restart the Mac.
   - In Zoom: Settings > Audio > Microphone > "BlackHole 2ch".
   Or get it from the website instead: go to
   https://existential.audio/blackhole/ , choose "BlackHole 2ch" and enter
   your email address; the download link arrives by email. Double-click
   the downloaded BlackHole2ch file, then do the last three steps above.

USE

- Open Cat Cam and click a cat (or press its number).
- In Zoom: Settings > Video > Camera > "OBS Virtual Camera". If it isn't
  listed, restart Zoom while Cat Cam is open.
- Keep a relaxed face for the first second. If the cat looks grumpy or
  turned while you are relaxed, press N.
- Raise your hands into view for paws. Turn on "Tail" in the top left corner
  of the Cat Cam window (or press T): then one hand on its own lifts the
  cat's tail, and both hands are two paws.
- Click "Paws" (or press H) to go from 3D paws to photo paws to "Real
  hands" and round again. Real hands are your own hands, cut out of the
  camera picture and tinted like your animal's fur; the call sees them, but
  nothing else of you. Light your hands well for a clean cut-out.
- "3D head" (or D) is on to begin with: a real 3D head. Turn it off for the
  flat photo head.
- Turn on "Background" (or press B) and pick a picture or video to put
  behind the animal. Turn it off and on again to pick another.
- With BlackHole installed, choose "BlackHole 2ch" as the microphone in
  Zoom (Settings > Audio > Microphone): the call hears your animal talking
  along while you talk, never your voice. Woofgang barks, and every cat has
  its own voice: trills, mews, meows, grumbles or yowls.
- "Voice" (or V) is on to begin with. Turn it off for the call to hear
  your own voice through BlackHole, and on again for the animal's.
- Sit still and quiet for a few seconds and your animal breathes and looks
  around on its own.
- Press 1 to 8 in the Cat Cam window for an emoji reaction next to your
  animal, which the call sees for a couple of seconds: 1 points at the
  viewer, 2 raises both hands, 3 folds them, 4 is a thumbs down, 5 claps,
  6 pinches the fingers, 7 raises a hand and 8 waves. (In the cat picker,
  the numbers pick a cat instead.)
- In the Cat Cam window: C picks another cat, Q quits.

TROUBLE?

- Black picture in Zoom: OBS is running. Quit OBS and reopen Cat Cam.
- "Cannot open webcam": allow Cat Cam under System Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Camera.
- The call hears your real voice: choose "BlackHole 2ch" as the
  microphone in Zoom. If BlackHole was just installed, restart the Mac.
- Anything else: everything Cat Cam does, the cameras it found and why
  one would not open, is written to ~/Library/Logs/Cat Cam.log (in
  Finder: Go > Go to Folder, paste that). Send it along when you ask
  for help. It starts afresh each time Cat Cam opens.
