CAT CAM - be a cat in your video calls
=======================================

INSTALL (once)

1. Drag "Cat Cam" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Install OBS Studio from https://obsproject.com (it provides the virtual
   camera Cat Cam sends your cat to). Then, once:
   - Open OBS and click "Start Virtual Camera".
   - If macOS asks, allow the system extension (System Settings >
     General > Login Items & Extensions).
   - Click "Stop Virtual Camera" and quit OBS.
   Keep OBS closed while you use Cat Cam.

3. Open Cat Cam from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't
   check the app (it isn't from the App Store). Click "Done", then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
   next to Cat Cam, and confirm. You only do this once.

4. Allow camera access when macOS asks.

5. A few keyboard shortcuts also work while Zoom is in front. For those,
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring and switch
   on Cat Cam (if it isn't listed, click + and choose Cat Cam from
   Applications). Then quit Cat Cam and open it again.

USE

- Open Cat Cam and click a cat (or press its number).
- In Zoom: Settings > Video > Camera > "OBS Virtual Camera". If it isn't
  listed, restart Zoom while Cat Cam is open.
- Keep a relaxed face for the first second. If the cat looks grumpy or
  turned while you are relaxed, press N.
- Raise your hands into view for paws. Tick "Tail" in the top left corner
  of the Cat Cam window (or press T) and your right hand lifts a tail.
- Stick your tongue out and the cat does too.
- In the Cat Cam window: C picks another cat, Q quits.

TROUBLE?

- Black picture in Zoom: OBS is running. Quit OBS and reopen Cat Cam.
- "Cannot open webcam": allow Cat Cam under System Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Camera.
- Errors are also written to ~/Library/Logs/Cat Cam.log
